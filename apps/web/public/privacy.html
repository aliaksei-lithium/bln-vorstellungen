BLN Vorstellungen

Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

BLN Vorstellungen (bln-vorstellungen.aliaksei-zhynhiarouski.workers.dev) is a free, non-commercial website by Aliaksei Zhynhiarouski in Berlin. It collects the programmes of Berlin theatres, concert venues, clubs and galleries into one list, and ranks the events for people who sign in by what they like. This policy explains what information the site collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it.

1. Information we collect

Visitors who do not sign in. No account and no personal information are needed. Display choices (which sources and categories to show, light or dark theme) are kept only in your browser’s local storage. Our hosting provider records technical request data (IP address, browser, time) for a short time to keep the service secure.

Invite requests. If you ask for an invite, we store the name, email address and note you enter, until the request is answered.

Signed-in users. Accounts are by invitation. For an account we store:

  • your name and email address, and a session cookie that keeps you signed in;
  • your taste description and settings (for example categories you want to see less of, a price limit);
  • the events you mark (interested, maybe, skipped, hidden) and your agenda;
  • photos or text you upload with Scan to add events, and the events read from them;
  • results computed for you: a score and a short reason per event, daily highlights and Discover pages.

Questions you ask in the Ask chat are sent to process the answer but are not stored; we only count how many you asked today, to apply a daily limit.

Usage counts. To see whether the site is used, we count per signed-in user and day how often the app was opened, how many pages of each kind (for example “event” or “agenda”) were shown, how many clicks were made and how many questions were asked. These are numbers only: not which events you opened, what you searched or what you asked. They are kept for 180 days, are seen only by the site owner, and no outside analytics service is used.

2. Google user data (YouTube)

Connecting YouTube Music is optional. If you connect it, you sign in with Google and grant read-only access (the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly scope). With that access we read:

  • the videos you liked (“Liked videos”, which includes songs you liked on YouTube Music): only the channel name and the category of each video, to recognise music artists;
  • the channels you subscribe to: their names and the topic categories YouTube gives them, to recognise which are music artists.

We store only:

  • the list of artist names derived from that information, with music genres for each;
  • the OAuth refresh token Google issues, so the list can be updated when you press “Update artists”.

We do not store the videos, playlists, watch history, comments or any other YouTube data, and we never post, change or delete anything in your Google or YouTube account.

How we use it. The artist list is used only to provide features to you: to rank upcoming events higher when they feature those artists or similar music, to show “My artists” in the event filters, and to show the list to you on your Me page. It is not shown to other users.

Sharing. Artist names and genres are sent to Anthropic (Claude), our AI service provider, only to name genres and to score events for you, as described in section 4. We do not sell Google user data, do not use it for advertising, do not transfer it to anyone else, and do not use it to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models.

Limited Use. BLN Vorstellungen’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access. “Disconnect” in YouTube Music on the Me page deletes the stored artist list and the token immediately. You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

3. Spotify data

Connecting Spotify is optional. With your permission we read your top and followed artists, and create a private playlist in your account only when you ask for one. We store the artist names with genres and the Spotify refresh token, use them in the same way as YouTube artists, and delete them when you disconnect.

4. Service providers

  • Cloudflare hosts the website, its database and file storage, and runs some AI models (Workers AI). Data is stored on Cloudflare’s infrastructure and encrypted at rest.
  • Anthropic (Claude), through Cloudflare’s AI Gateway, processes event descriptions, your taste description and your artist names to read event listings, score events for you, write daily highlights and answer Ask questions; it also performs web searches for Ask and highlights. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models.
  • Google and Spotify, only if you connect them, as described above.

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for advertising. There are no third-party trackers or analytics on this site.

5. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. OAuth tokens are kept on the server and never sent to your browser. Access to the database is limited to the site’s operator.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

Account data is kept while you have an account. Disconnecting Google or Spotify deletes what came from them at once. To delete your account and everything stored with it, or to receive a copy of your data, email the address below; we do it within 30 days. Images of past events are deleted two days after the event.

7. Children

The site is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Significant changes are announced on the site.
