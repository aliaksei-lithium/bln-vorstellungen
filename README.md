Berlin has hundreds of stages, and each one publishes its programme its own way: a calendar feed here, a Spielplan
page there, a Luma list somewhere else. BLN Vorstellungen reads them all and turns them into one calm list. Tell it
in a sentence what you like, and it ranks every upcoming event for you and says why.

## What makes it good

- **One list for the whole city.** Theatre, opera, concerts, clubs and talks side by side. A production with twelve
  performances is one entry, not twelve rows.
- **AI reads the venue pages.** Claude reads Spielplan pages that have no feed: every date, price and room.
- **Ranked for your taste.** Describe what you like in a sentence; every event gets a score and a one-line reason.
  Swipe to sort.
- **Search by meaning.** "Something fun with kids" finds the Kinder Rave.
- **Agenda in your calendar.** Your picks by day, clashes flagged, synced to Google or Apple Calendar.
- **Made for Berlin.** German and English, "Fr 30.10. · 20:00", "ab 18 €", "frei". Light and dark.

## Where the events come from

The Left Berlin · Luma Berlin · Maxim Gorki Theater · Berliner Ensemble · Deutsches Theater · Staatsoper Unter den
Linden · A-Trane · Privatclub · Bar jeder Vernunft. Each is checked on its own schedule, every 6 to 24 hours.
Adding a venue is one entry, not new code.
